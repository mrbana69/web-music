Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Preluded ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our web music player accessible at https://preluded.vercel.app.

1. Information We Collect

Preluded practices strict data minimization:

  • Google OAuth Identity Data: When you sign in with Google, we receive basic identity profile information (name, email address, profile picture, and user ID) via standard OAuth scopes (openid, profile, email).
  • Local Device Storage (LocalStorage): Your listening history, customized playlists, and favorite songs ("Liked Songs") are stored locally within your browser's LocalStorage on your own device.
  • No Financial Data: Preluded does not collect, process, or store credit card numbers, billing information, or payment credentials.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the minimal information collected solely for the following technical and functional purposes:

  • To authenticate your session securely via Google Sign-In.
  • To provide personalized music features such as your "Daily Mix" and playlist management.
  • To enable continuous streaming playback and synchronized lyrics.

Zero Data Selling: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or share user personal data with any third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing platforms.

3. Third-Party API Services (Google & YouTube)

Preluded utilizes YouTube API Services and Innertube endpoints to search and play publicly available audio content. By using Preluded, you agree to be bound by:

  • YouTube Terms of Service
  • Google Privacy Policy

4. Revoking Google Account Access

You can revoke Preluded's access to your Google account at any time via the official Google Security settings page:

https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

5. Data Deletion

You can instantly delete all locally stored playlists, session tokens, and preferences by signing out through the Library tab or clearing your browser's storage.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: preluded.app@gmail.com.

Informativa sulla Privacy

Ultimo aggiornamento: 27 Agosto 2026

La presente Informativa descrive come Preluded raccoglie, utilizza e protegge i dati degli utenti durante l'utilizzo dell'applicazione all'indirizzo https://preluded.vercel.app.

1. Quali dati raccogliamo

  • Dati di Autenticazione Google OAuth: Nome visualizzato, email e ID account tramite permessi standard (openid, profile, email).
